# Life by Design — Visual Language

This is a lightweight art-direction brief for Claude Design and future UI work. It draws energy from the *Designing Your Life* cover without reproducing its composition.

## Creative idea

**A field guide at the edge of a lake.** The interface should feel like someone opened a beautiful book outdoors and began sketching possible routes. Calm expanses hold small, bright marks of possibility.

## Palette

- **Warm paper** — `oklch(96% 0.023 83)`: the default ground; human and editorial.
- **Lake** — `oklch(78% 0.102 193)`: immersive teaching and reflection surfaces.
- **Deep pine** — `oklch(36% 0.075 169)`: nature, groundedness, alternate emphasis.
- **Ink** — `oklch(23% 0.035 190)`: text and primary actions; never pure black.
- **Sun yellow** — `oklch(88% 0.182 97)`: curiosity and forward movement.
- **Trail red** — `oklch(60% 0.205 29)`: a rare marker for action and energy.
- **Clear blue** — `oklch(70% 0.14 239)`: one possible path among several.

Use paper and lake for most visual weight. Bright colors appear as dots, route markers, focus, and key actions—not large decorative gradients.

## Type

- Display: a literary old-style serif with personality, high contrast, and a warm italic.
- Body: a humane geometric sans with open counters and excellent small-size readability.
- Headlines are large, left-aligned, and composed like book covers. Labels use spaced small caps sparingly.

## Forms and interactions

- Prefer lines, open space, and canvases over stacks of rounded cards.
- Every concept invites one small action nearby.
- Inputs resemble thoughtful annotations rather than enterprise forms.
- Motion is slow enough to feel calm but never theatrical; animate only opacity and transforms.
- Notes persist locally and the interface says so plainly.

## Image prompts

### Hero landscape

> Wide editorial illustration of an expansive alpine lake at first light, layered pine-green mountains, pale turquoise water, warm cream sky, quiet contemplative atmosphere, a few tiny primary-color trail markers drifting like constellations, sophisticated screen-print texture, minimal shapes, generous negative space, no people, no text, premium book-jacket art direction, landscape 4:5 crop with subject weighted toward lower right.

### Section texture

> Subtle warm recycled-paper grain with faint cyan fibers, nearly seamless, tactile but restrained, flat even lighting, no objects, no text.

## Avoid

- Generic wellness photography, yoga poses, journals beside coffee, and stock-photo sunsets.
- Neon gradients, glassmorphism, floating dashboard cards, or startup-style feature grids.
- Literal replicas of the book cover or its dot-ring composition.
- Language implying there is one purpose, correct choice, or optimized version of a life.
